📊 HR Analytics Dashboard
Turning employee data into actionable workforce insights with Power
BI.

An interactive dashboard to explore employee demographics, attrition,
salary, job satisfaction, and experience.

✨ Highlights
- 👥 1,481 employee records analyzed across 37 attributes
- 🧹 Data cleaned in Power Query, including duplicate and
  inconsistent-value handling
- 📌 6 DAX-driven KPIs for workforce and attrition tracking
- 📈 7 interactive visuals with Department and Age Group slicers
- 🔎 Explore attrition by department, salary band, gender, job role,
  age, and experience
  
🛠️ Tech Stack
Power BI · Power Query · DAX · Data Visualization

🚀 Get Started
1. Download HR analytics dashboard.pbix.
2. Open it in Microsoft Power BI Desktop.
3. Use the Department and Age Group slicers to explore the
   data.
4. Select or hover over visuals to inspect details and discover
   patterns.
   
🎯 Project Goal
Help HR teams understand workforce patterns and identify areas for
further investigation---supporting more informed, data-driven decisions.
